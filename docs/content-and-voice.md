# Content and voice

## Character

OpenE2EE is calm, exact, candid, and developer-native. It explains boundaries
instead of borrowing credibility from dramatic security language.

## Writing rules

- Lead with the user outcome.
- Name the actor responsible for storage, transport, identity, or recovery.
- Prefer concrete verbs such as `encrypt`, `verify`, `store`, and `rotate`.
- Explain tradeoffs and failure states directly.
- Use short labels and complete sentences for guidance.
- Avoid “military-grade,” “unbreakable,” “zero risk,” and similar absolutes.

## Naming

- `OpenE2EE` — organization and brand
- `OpenE2EE SDK` — primary product
- `OpenE2EE Docs` — documentation surface
- `OpenE2EE Console` — licensing and account surface
- `Open E2EE LLC` — legal entity only

Use `end-to-end encrypted` in prose when describing a property. Use `E2EE`
when space is constrained or the audience already understands the term.
