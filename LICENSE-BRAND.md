# Brand asset license

**All rights reserved. The OpenE2EE visual identity is not open source.**

The Apache License 2.0 in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the code in this
repository. It does **not** cover the visual identity, which is reserved.

## What is reserved

Everything that is the identity rather than the machinery:

- `brand/source/` — the canonical mark geometry, lockup construction, and
  social card definitions.
- `brand/generated/` — every mark, favicon, lockup, presentation sheet, and
  social card built from those sources, in every format and mode.
- `packages/design/dist/assets/` — the same assets as published to npm.
- The names **OpenE2EE** and **Open E2EE**, the carrier mark, and the
  `Open` + `E2EE` wordmark, in any weight, colour, or arrangement.

## What is not reserved

The design tokens, CSS, theme module, diagram grammar, CLI, build scripts,
tests, and documentation are Apache-2.0. You may build a product on the token
system and the diagram grammar without touching the identity. In particular,
the diagram primitives draw a carrier bracket that is geometrically the same
construction as the mark — using them to draw *diagrams* is exactly what they
are for; using them to reconstruct the *mark* is not.

## What you may do with the reserved assets

Without asking:

- Reproduce the mark and lockups unmodified to refer to OpenE2EE — in an
  article, a talk, a comparison table, a list of dependencies, a "built with"
  badge.
- Redistribute them as an unmodified part of the `@open-e2ee/design` package.

Subject to `DESIGN.md`, which is the usage contract: the clear space, the size
rule, and the misuse list apply to every reproduction.

## What you may not do

- Modify, recolour, redraw, animate, or extend the mark or wordmark.
- Use them as your own identity, or in a way that suggests OpenE2EE endorses,
  sponsors, or authored something it did not.
- Use them in a fork or derivative product without replacing the identity
  first. Fork the code freely; the fork needs its own name and its own mark.

## Trademark status

No trademark registration has been filed. The identity is reserved rather than
released while name clearance is outstanding, because releasing it is not
reversible and clearance may yet force a rename. This file is the reason the
reservation exists, not a permanent position — if you are blocked by it, ask.
